## 8. Questions for the Authors (as a PC Member)

1. **Dialogue vs. Static Form Baseline:** Since MURENA has a fixed 6-dimension schema $(I, D, L, C_b, C_e, C_p)$, what concrete advantage does multi-turn natural-language clarification offer over a hybrid UI that parses the initial free-text query into a 6-field editable form with explicit *"No preference"* checkboxes?
2. **Role of EVPI Ordering on Open Omissions:** Given that open omission questions have empty option lists (`alternatives: []`) and therefore receive $\text{EVPI} = 0$, the SAGE-Agent EVPI score only prioritizes option-bearing ambiguity questions (e.g., city disambiguation with $1 - 1/n > 0$) ahead of omissions and penalizes re-asking the same dimension after a vague reply ($-\lambda \cdot \text{count}$). Have you tested cases where an initial request simultaneously contains both a location/use ambiguity and unconfirmed omissions to demonstrate this prioritization live?
3. **Cross-Model Oracle Ceiling Difference (`0.87` vs. `0.70`):** In Section 5, oracle self-agreement on top-5 Jaccard is `0.87 [0.79, 0.95]` for Claude Haiku 5.5 (`missingness_v3_haiku_r2`) but `0.70 [0.51, 0.89]` for `gpt-6-luna` (`imperfect_user_codex_vlast`). Since the two series also used different property-table snapshots (`bc6732c8...` vs. `d3956041...`), how much of the lower noise ceiling in the Codex series comes from LLM extraction/scoring variance versus differences in the underlying property snapshot?
4. **Downstream Agent Inference After Explicit Opt-Out:** In the opt-out runs, the intent-mean rate with no filter atom on the declined dimension is `0.90 [0.71, 1.00]` (95/105 runs), because in 10 runs where the user explicitly declined service proximity ($C_p$), the inherited `ProximityAgent` still inferred an education proximity filter from the intended use in $\hat{q}$. Would passing the confirmed dimension-state map (`Cp = NOT_REQUESTED`) directly to the orchestrator to suppress downstream extraction for declined dimensions be desirable, or do you deliberately restrict the interface to the rewritten text $\hat{q}$?
