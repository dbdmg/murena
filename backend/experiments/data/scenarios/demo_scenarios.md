# Suggested two EDBT demo walkthroughs

These are **illustrative scripts**. Actual SAGE question wording and selection order are generated by the LLM at runtime and may differ. The system is not claimed to have produced the samples below.

## Guided: disambiguate elderly care (`UC20_senior_care_disambiguation`)

**Initial user request**

> Find a hotel building for an elder-care facility in Turin in energy class C or better near healthcare services.

**What should happen**

The intended-use dimension `D` is underspecified: day care and residential care are materially different services; `Cb` is also missing. Demonstrate the system asking the user to identify the intended destination and capacity, without changing the current property type (`I=hotel`).

**Example answers for the audience or simulated user**

- `D`: “The intended use is conversion into a residential senior care facility (RSA) with overnight stays, not a senior day center.”
- `Cb`: “The building capacity and area requirement are for 30 residents, with at least 1500 sqm of gross floor area.”

**Show** the generated clarified query before execution; inspect the `matched_use_cases` entry `Senior Residential Care (RSA)`; explain that the RSA bedroom minima refer to *individual rooms*, not gross property area. Then display the candidates, multi-agent scores and map **if the configured dataset returns results**.

## Open-ended: elicit a funded student-housing project (`UC19_student_grant_multiturn`)

**Initial user request**

> Find an office building in Turin in energy class E or worse, for renovation near public transport.

**What should happen**

The user states current property type, location, energy direction and relevant services; `D` and `Cb` are missing. SAGE should ask about the future destination and useful building constraints, then produce a final request without changing previously stated preferences.

**Example answers**

- `D`: “I intend to convert it into a 45-bed university student residence under the specific CDP/PNRR funding program.”
- `Cb`: “I need a property with at least 700 sqm of gross floor area.”

**Show** the difference between one-shot and clarified query, the explicit grant applicability, the retrieved candidates and the scoring explanation. Stress that CDP/PNRR dimensional standards are grant-specific, not universal whole-property gross-area cutoffs.

**Optional alternative:** Use `UC21_childcare_disambiguation` instead to show the model choosing the `Spring section (Sezione primavera)` record within a broader child-services request, while preserving the hosting kindergarten condition.
